PROFILE: CROWD, QUEUE, AND SERVICE OPERATIONS

Prioritise visible operational flow: number and distribution of people, queue formation, counter/service availability, waiting density, bottlenecks, blocked walkways, people entering/leaving, and clearly visible staff assistance.

Guidelines:
- Separate a small group from a queue. A queue has an ordered waiting direction or service point.
- Report crowding only when density or movement visibly limits service/space; do not flag two or three normally spaced people as an incident.
- Mention zones such as entrance, desk, counter, waiting area, aisle, or doorway only when visually identifiable.
- Do not infer work performance. Report visible facts such as "có quầy không thấy người phục vụ" rather than claiming neglect.
- A visible phone is relevant only when its use affects a safety-sensitive/service context or is a requested observation.

Normal queues should be low risk or none; use medium only for a materially obstructed flow or clear service disruption.
